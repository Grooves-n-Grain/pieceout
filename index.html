Legend

Trace colours

  • Red box: island, a black piece not attached to the rest; it falls out
  • Grey box with pin: kept island (left as a separate piece)
  • Orange box: thin feature, narrower than the kerf
  • Green line: bridge
  • Cyan line: traced outline
  • 1 Number: island id, stable for the life of the image

Selecting islands

Click an island (or its row in the list) to select just that one. Shift+click adds or removes it from the current selection instead of replacing it. Ctrl+drag on the canvas draws a box and selects every island fully inside it (Shift+drag adds to the selection). Selecting more than one island opens a group popover to bridge, delete, or keep them together. Esc clears the selection.

Trace settings

Threshold: the grey level that splits the image into black (material) and white (cut away). Lower it to keep more dark detail, raise it to drop faint greys.

Art is white on black: tick this when your material is the white part of the image and the black is what gets cut away.

Smoothness: how much the traced curves are allowed to drift from the pixels. Low keeps every jaggy; high gives smoother, simpler paths.

Min speck size: black specks and white pinholes smaller than this many pixels are dropped before tracing.

Kerf / bit: the width your laser or bit removes. Features narrower than this get an orange box because they may burn or break away.

Bridge width: how wide the tabs that hold an island in place are. A small bridge just needs to hold until the piece is glued, then it snaps off.

Delete islands under: islands smaller than this area are removed automatically. 0 turns the rule off; kept islands are never removed.

Width: the real-world width of the finished piece. Height follows the image's proportions. Fit to panel uses a Layout panel's safe area instead.

Include outline: also cut the outer rectangle of the image. Off when you only want the shapes inside, or when a Layout panel already supplies the outline.

Layer keywords (Preview)

CUT: profile, cut, through, outline, cutout, cut-out, contour, outside, border, perimeter, laser, drill.

POCKET: pocket, engrave, carve, vcarve, v-carve, texture, relief.

Ignored: names starting with "guide".

Anything else is ignored until you click it; your clicks are remembered per layer name.
